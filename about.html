OUR STORY & MISSION

Uniting somatic movement, mental wellness, and companion animal rescue.

HOW IT BEGAN

What began as a collaborative foster initiative between a yoga instructor and a shelter veterinarian has blossomed into the city's premier animal wellness sanctuary.

Founded by Maya Chen and Dr. Elena Ramos, our studio was created to solve two interconnected challenges: urban stress and the need for peaceful, cage-free socialization spaces for shelter rescue cats. By merging somatic movement with the scientifically proven calming benefits of cat purrs, we offer an environment where both humans and felines thrive.
