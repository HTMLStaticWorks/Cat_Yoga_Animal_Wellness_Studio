OUR STORY & MISSION

Uniting somatic movement, mental wellness, and companion animal rescue.

HOW IT BEGAN

What began as a collaborative foster initiative between a yoga instructor and a shelter veterinarian has blossomed into the city's premier cat yoga and animal wellness sanctuary.

The studio keeps and cares for cats that roam freely during yoga sessions, allowing visitors to interact with them. Founded by Maya Chen and Dr. Elena Ramos, our cat-care sanctuary bridges mindful human wellness with companion rescue. Gentle somatic movement releases bodily tension, while affectionate roaming cats and their soothing 20–140 Hz purrs create a calming, comfortable environment that relieves stress and deepens relaxation.
